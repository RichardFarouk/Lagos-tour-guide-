Visit Lagos

Enjoy culture and sports in this vibrant district close to the center of Victoria Island.

Top three activities to do in Lagos

Attend a show or a concert

The National Theatre is a great place to spend your night out.

Go for a picnic in the beach

The Beach is perfect for picnics, playing some fun, or just chilling out.

Get sweaty at the sports field

Play volleyball, football, basketball or lift some weight.
